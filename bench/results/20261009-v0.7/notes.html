How this set was measured (and what stopped it)

  • Data-loss bug found and fixed mid-run. The first FluxMQ 1-node pass (build acba45c) stopped on its own id check: in fluxmq-single-sat-1024B-1p1c-r3, 1,069,078 acknowledged messages were neither delivered nor counted as retention skips. Root cause: when a lagging group's catch-up read-ahead was continued by the next prefetch, install_fetch dropped the held read-ahead and the dispatcher skipped the gap as "a hole in the log" — stored, undelivered messages skipped silently. Fixed in 4533c6c with a property test (crates/fluxmq-core/tests/retention_overtake.rs); every skip of undelivered messages is now warned and counted. All FluxMQ rows of this set were measured again from scratch on the fixed build; the pre-fix rows are kept in investigation/v07-pre-fix-retention-skip/.
  • Harness audit cap. One lz4 run stopped because the FluxMQ audit (re-reading the log to classify misses) hit its 120 s cap before the end of a ~56M-message compressed backlog; nothing had been removed and the consumer was still draining. The cap was raised to 600 s and the lz4/cumulative rows were rerun; the aborted run is in investigation/audit-cap-120s/.
  • Disk guard. The 3-node fsync=os rows stopped once at 9.9 GB host free (the Docker VM disk grows faster than it is given back); they were rerun after freeing space (investigation/cluster-os-disk-abort/).
  • Crash matrix in two parts. The device-cleanup helper hung on a package download after the FluxMQ group-commit systems; the remaining systems (FluxMQ 3-node fsync=os, all Kafka) ran in a second invocation into the same directory (crash/env.json records the second command). The helper now uses the crash image (no download).
  • Excluded runs. Kafka at 100 B 4×4 where the harness container was CPU-bound and its measurement window stretched past 1.5× the plan (5 of 6 at 3 nodes, 1 of 2 at 1 node) are excluded from medians and kept on record. FluxMQ's 1 KiB / 100 B rows where producers outran consumers are reported as measured ("not drained": every miss was either still stored or a counted retention skip — 0 unexplained).
  • Reductions to fit the time budget: 2 saturation repeats (v0.6: 3 on one node), catch-up and overload only on the headline systems (FluxMQ, Kafka ± fsync, RabbitMQ), lz4 / cumulative rows on one node only, 2 crash iterations per mode and target, RabbitMQ catch-up backlog 100 MiB, failover 2 runs per system, pipeline A/B 2 repeats in one order.
  • After quick fixes. One small fix found by profiling (raw-run compaction deferred to the end of an ack batch) was measured afterwards in after-quick-fixes/; the headline tables stay on the matrix build.
  • Commit pipeline A/B (investigation/pipeline-ab/, 1 KiB 4×4, 2 repeats; 10k msg/s latency, one run): pipeline on published +22 % (440k vs 368k) but consumed −11 % (268k vs 303k), p99 22 vs 14 ms. Recommendation: keep FLUXMQ_COMMIT_PIPELINE off.
