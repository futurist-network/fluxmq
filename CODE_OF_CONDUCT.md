# Code of conduct

FluxMQ follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, discussions, pull requests or
any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub](https://github.com/futurist-network/fluxmq/security/advisories/new) or by opening a
discussion asking for a private contact. Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers may
remove content and block participants who break these rules.
